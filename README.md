# Richter Biosystems AG
### Direct-From-Factory European Genomics Instrumentation

A commercial web platform for **Richter Biosystems AG** (Basel, Switzerland & Jena, Germany), precision manufacturers of high-throughput Next-Generation Sequencers, automated gene synthesizers, droplet digital PCR, and single-cell robotics platforms.

---

## Key Features & Highlights

1. **Direct-From-Fabrication Model**:
   - Zero commercial intermediary/dealer markups.
   - Direct factory allocation, in-house optical laser calibration, and notarized European factory guarantees.

2. **Technical Specifications & Real Equipment Imagery**:
   - Real, authentic laboratory instrument photography.
   - Comprehensive technical parameters: laser wavelengths, sCMOS sensor resolution, throughput per run, fluidic flow rates, cycle times, mechanical repeatability, and cleanroom footprint.
   - Folded-by-default architecture: full technical specifications, cleanroom photography, and direct procurement channels expand upon selecting each machine.

3. **Catalogue & Categories**:
   - **DNA Sequencing Systems**: Massively parallel sequencers (`VR-900 Prominence™`, `VR-600 NovaBench™`).
   - **Gene & Oligo Synthesis**: Solid-phase phosphoramidite synthesizers (`GS-400 OligoSynthetix™`, `GS-200 CompactSynth™`).
   - **Digital PCR Analysis**: Droplet digital PCR systems (`DP-200 AbsoluteQuant™`, `DP-100 BenchQuant™`, `DP-400 MultiPlex™`).
   - **Single-Cell Genomics**: Automated robotic platforms (`SC-600 CellMatrix™`, `SC-300 MicroDrop™`, `SC-800 MegaCell™`).

4. **Sole Official Contact Channel**:
   - **Telegram Bot**: [@Slava_Dev_bot](https://t.me/Slava_Dev_bot)
   - **SLA**: Answering client messages in 24 hours.

5. **Security & Anti-AI Protection**:
   - Strict Content Security Policy (CSP) and HTTP defense headers (`X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Permissions-Policy`).
   - Human authorship verification and TDM reservations pursuant to Directive (EU) 2019/790, Article 4(3).
